# Canvas Business Builder
**AI-assisted Business Model Canvas Platform**

> Decision-grade tooling for CEOs, Boards, Advisors, and Investors.

---

## 🧠 Overview

**Canvas Business Builder** is a web-based application designed to help business leaders **design, assess, and evolve business models** using the Business Model Canvas framework, enhanced with structured analytics and AI-assisted insights.

The product is conceived as a **board-ready tool**, prioritizing:
- clarity over complexity  
- explainability over black-box scoring  
- human judgment supported (not replaced) by AI  

This repository contains the **frontend application** and serves as the **single source of truth** for the product.

---

## 🎯 Target Users

- CEOs and founders  
- Board members and Chairs  
- Business advisors and consultants  
- Investment funds and venture builders

## 🧩 Core Capabilities (Current & Planned)

### Current
- React-based Canvas application (Vite)
- Modular frontend structure
- Tailwind-based UI foundation

### Planned / In Progress
- Business Model Canvas editor (9 blocks)
- Health Panel by dimensions (Completeness / Coherence / Risk)
- Configurable scoring by business type (SaaS, Industrial, Services, etc.)
- AI-assisted qualitative insights
- Versioning and audit trail
- SaaS plans logic (Free / Pro / Team)
